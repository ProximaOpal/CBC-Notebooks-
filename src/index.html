Your data

Privacy Policy

CBC Notebooks (“we”) provides CBC learning resources for Kenya Grades 4–9. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the website or installed app.

What we collect

If you add or request a resource, we collect the name, email and details you type in that form. The installed app may store pages and images on your device so they work offline. We do not require an account to browse subjects.

How we use it

Form details are used only to review contributions, fulfil requests, and reply to you. We do not sell learner or parent data. We do not use the forms for advertising.

Children

The service is designed for Grades 4–9 with parents and teachers. Do not submit another child’s personal data beyond what is needed for a resource request.

Kenya law

We aim to handle personal data in line with the Data Protection Act, 2019. You may ask to access, correct or delete information you sent through a form by emailing the address you used in that form.

Access and erasure (ODPC)

Data subjects may request an export or deletion of stored account records. Those requests are handled through /api/privacy/export and /api/privacy/delete after identity verification. Telemetry is stored without names, emails, phone numbers or full IP addresses.

Cookies and install

The Progressive Web App uses a service worker to cache lesson pages on your device. You can remove the app and its cache from your browser or phone settings at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@cbcnotebooks.co.ke
